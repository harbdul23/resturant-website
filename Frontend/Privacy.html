Who we are

What we collect

  • Account: your name, phone number, optional email and a password. The password is stored only in scrambled form, so we cannot read it.
  • Orders: the dishes you order, the total, whether it is pickup or delivery, your delivery address and any note you add.
  • Reservations: your name, phone number, date, time, number of guests and any note.
  • On your device: your browser keeps your cart, your login and the private codes for your orders, so the site can remember them.
  • Technical: your internet address is used briefly to block repeated failed attempts and abuse.

Why we use it

To prepare and deliver your orders, confirm your reservations, contact you about them by phone or WhatsApp, keep your account and order history, and protect the site from misuse.

Who can see it

  • Our staff who handle orders and reservations.
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the website's data on its servers.
  • WhatsApp, only if you tap a WhatsApp button. Their own privacy policy applies from that point.
  • Google, which supplies the map on our Contact section.

We do not sell your information and the site does not use advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

We keep orders and reservations for as long as we need them for our records. Your account stays until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you by contacting us using the details above. Logging out removes your login from the device you are using. You can also clear your browser's site data at any time.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website can promise perfect security. Please choose a password you do not use elsewhere.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
