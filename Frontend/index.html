QUESTIONS

Good to know

Do you deliver?

Yes. Choose Delivery in your cart, enter your address and add a note if you like. The delivery fee is shown before you place the order. If we can't reach your area, we'll call or message you.

How do I pay?

You pay the restaurant when you collect or receive your order.

How do I reserve a table?

Use our reservation page. Choose a date, time and number of guests, and we'll confirm by phone or WhatsApp.

I have an allergy. What should I do?

Please tell us in the order note or call us before ordering. Our kitchen handles many ingredients, so we can't promise any dish is free of a particular ingredient.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Call or WhatsApp us as soon as possible on +234 814 133 9830. Once the kitchen has started preparing your food, we may not be able to change it.

Do I need an account to order?

No. An account just saves your details and shows your order history on any device.
